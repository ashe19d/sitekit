Privacy Policy

Vexora SiteKit is a website builder. This page explains what information it uses. Last updated: October 2026.

What we collect

  • Account details. If you create an account with email, we store your email address and a salted, hashed version of your password (we never store the password itself).
  • Google or GitHub sign-in. If you sign in with Google or GitHub, we receive your account ID, email address, name and profile picture. We use them only to identify your account and show your name in the app.
  • Sign-in records. We keep the date of your first and most recent sign-in and how many times you signed in.

What stays on your device

The websites you build (text, colors, settings) are saved in your own browser on your device. We do not receive or store them on our servers.

GitHub export

If you use "Export to GitHub", your GitHub access token is kept only in your browser tab for that session and is sent only to GitHub to create your repository and upload your files. We do not store it on our servers.

How we use and share information

We use your information only to run sign-in and keep your account secure. We do not sell it, rent it, or use it for advertising. Account data is stored with our hosting provider, Cloudflare. We share it with no one else unless the law requires it.

Your choices

You can ask us to delete your account and the data we hold about you at any time by contacting us through github.com/ashe19d. You can also revoke Google or GitHub access from your Google or GitHub account settings.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update this page and the date above.
